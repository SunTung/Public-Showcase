# Novelty and Scope Statement

## Scope

The manuscript proves nonexistence only for the restricted fixed-endpoint-colour simple `K6,D=3` model with one primitive edge per unordered vertex pair. It does not cover the unrestricted multi-edge-type Krenn-Gu model and does not claim to resolve the general conjecture.

## Relation to stronger existing work

A public 2026 Lean-checked artifact proves the stronger unrestricted `(6,3)` nonexistence theorem. Accordingly, the present manuscript is not positioned as a new parameter-range result or a priority claim over that theorem.

## Claimed contribution

The contribution is explanatory and certificate-oriented:

1. a short analytic reduction from the three monochromatic normalization equations to three pairwise-edge-disjoint nonzero witnesses and a nine-edge nonzero backbone;
2. a direct combinatorial classification of the 80 witness triples into 60 triangular-prism and 20 `K3,3` unions;
3. a symmetry reduction of the six optional edges to 23 support orbits;
4. a structural obstruction: every admissible completion forces a mixed inherited-colouring fiber containing exactly one nonzero monomial;
5. two materially different exact verifier programs reproducing the finite impossibility, including symbolic contradiction cores of size at most three.

The intended value is a small, transparent, human-followable explanation of why cancellation fails in this restricted calibration model.
